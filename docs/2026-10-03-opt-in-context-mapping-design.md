# Explicit, versioned reuse of reviewed context

## Supported increment

Add immutable `mapping_version` records for explicitly opted-in property-ID or meter-number mappings to a reviewed whole-property WEG/MIET scope. Current master data and invoice extraction do not establish trustworthy customer/contract identifiers or supplier namespaces; those remain unsupported. Supplier names/addresses never become mapping keys. Reused mappings annotate bounded context candidates and remain manual proposals. They do not select a scope, accept fields, clear exceptions, publish originals or create costs.

A mapping is a reusable context hint, not proof that a future invoice belongs to a scope. In particular, a property ID alone does not distinguish WEG from MIET costs, and a meter number does not establish dated installation validity or whole-property responsibility. Keep other direct evidence and alternative scopes visible. A matching saved mapping never overrides contrary source assertions.

## Evidence, versioning and consent

Create or reactivate only from an exact current supported allocation adoption and its field/context/source chain. The selected identifier must occur on the pinned extracted page under the supported explicit label, and match the adopted property's canonical UUID or currently recorded meter association. The database verifies the exact occurrence and master association; client-supplied parsed evidence is insufficient. Retain the page quotation, extraction, context and adoption privately.

The user explicitly chooses the identifier and future service-date interval, reviews the target property/scope and acknowledges reuse of this identifier-to-scope association by authorized users of that property. Nothing is learned automatically from editing or adoption. Do not default the consent checkbox or infer a future interval from one invoice. Require current source prepare/export and destination prepare, with property-master read and the scope's full supported interval. Invalid or ambiguous evidence cannot produce a mapping.

Version chains are scoped to organization, property, identifier kind and exact normalized value. Every version pins its target scope and effective service-date interval, actor, reason and private source basis. Append active or revoked state with exact predecessor and idempotency key under the existing organization financial/ACL lock order. Revocation stops future candidate reuse even for a document whose dates precede revocation; the history remains. Reactivation requires fresh supported source proof and new explicit consent. Target changes require authority over all retained scopes. Never mutate a confirmed cost or historical mapping.

Activation retries recheck current authority over the request's private source and all retained targets before returning its exact sanitized receipt; they never reactivate a revoked chain. Revocation requires prepare authority over all retained targets and an explicit reason, but does not require the original uploader's private intake access. This lets authorized property staff stop reuse after the uploader leaves. A subsequent activation uses its own exact current source proof; it does not grant access to earlier private origins. Preserve old and new targets through composite organization/property FKs and immutable lineage constraints. Downgrade is blocked once evidence exists, and coverage manifests include private retained mapping dependencies.

## Audience separation and candidate reuse

Sanitized mapping descriptors contain identifier, target property/scope, interval, state, policy and version. The opt-in explicitly authorizes this narrow reuse; it does not publish private intake quotations, source IDs, context IDs, request keys or digests. Separate source-owner detail/commands from property/scope-authorized candidate descriptors. A candidate consumer need not inherit access to the original creator's private intake. All retained target scopes and current property-master authority remain required for descriptors. Each activation's private source dependencies remain included in its source-owner command/retry access and access-transition coverage. Target-authorized revocation and sanitized history remain possible independently.

Candidate discovery applies its existing source and destination filtering before mapping lookup and caps. Reuse requires an exact labelled identifier on the new source, a latest active mapping, the full extracted service interval within mapping and scope dates, and a still-valid canonical property/meter association. Missing dates, revoked/expired mapping, master drift or ambiguity preserve manual review without a uniqueness claim. Expose the mapping version and readable target/effective interval, never private origin evidence. Any copied mapping basis must remain identifiable in the separately submitted manual proposal reason; it never upgrades that proposal's review state.

## Qualification

Require no implicit learning, explicit consent and future interval, exact pinned page proof, source hash/current-head checks, unsupported identifiers, scope/period mismatch, meter reassignment, private-origin isolation with authorized second-user reuse, source/export/prepare revocation, all-retained-target authority, append-only chain sealing, concurrent predecessor race, exact unknown-command retry after revocation, changed-body conflicts, role/pause enforcement, coverage and guarded migration roundtrip. Candidate/UI qualification must show saved basis, preserve contrary alternatives, avoid automatic selection, retain unknown requests and clear denied private evidence.

Reactivation may revalidate the same still-current adopted original; it does not require a different original. A new consent and explicit predecessor are always required. Meter activation pins one exact row and unit association and rejects duplicate numbers within the property. Meter writes serialize with mapping commands; a subsequent reassignment, renumbering or duplicate makes reuse ineligible. Identical numbers across properties remain independent alternatives. Reasons and quotations remain private even in sanitized history.

The copied mapping reference in a manual proposal reason is editable review context, not an enforced dependency or proof of accepted mapping authority. Reuse never grants a financial or resolution effect.

## Implemented boundary

Migration 0112 stores immutable active/revoked versions in a private table with no runtime table grants. Composite organization/property/identifier predecessor constraints, a lineage trigger, explicit request digests and the organization financial/ACL locks seal each chain. The public projection is an explicit JSON allowlist. No private reasons, quotations, origin IDs, actors, request keys or digests enter candidate descriptors. Access coverage includes every retained mapping row; populated downgrade is refused.

The database independently parses bounded whole labelled lines from the exact pinned extraction attempt. Property IDs canonicalize as UUIDs; meter numbers remain case-sensitive. One exact meter row and unit association are pinned privately. All meter inserts, updates and deletes serialize with mapping commands, including insertion of a duplicate number. Reuse rechecks current scope interval and master association; it remains advisory and retains contradictory source assertions and other candidate scopes.

Activation checks source prepare/export, selected target, destination prepare and every retained target before historical replay. It deliberately does not require financial confirmation capability. Fresh activation additionally validates current review heads and the exact source/master proof. The options endpoint independently authorizes a retained identifier before evaluating freshness: an inaccessible newer context disables new activation without erasing an otherwise authorized uncertain historical request. Revocation uses retained-target authority without the old private uploader's source access. Activation and revocation replays return their exact historical receipt without changing the current head.

The source UI requires explicit identifier selection, blank service-date inputs, a reason and unchecked reuse consent. Changes reset consent. The separate mapping detail page distinguishes the requested historical version from the fresh current state and permits explicit revocation. Both forms retain exact uncertain requests; actual denial clears retained evidence. Saved activation receipts retain their own authorization basis even if the parent result disappears, and selecting another identifier clears the previous receipt. Candidate copying preserves the user's reason and visibly appends one saved-version reference on separate manual submission; a combined reason longer than the existing limit is blocked rather than truncated.
