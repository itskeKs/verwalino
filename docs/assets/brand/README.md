# Verwalino logo

The “Lift” wordmark is the logo. Its asymmetric V has a lifted right arm and a
flat, rounded foot. The V alone is the standalone mark for app icons and favicons.

## Files

- `verwalino-wordmark.svg`: ink wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `verwalino-wordmark-dark.svg`: light wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `verwalino-mark.svg`: ink standalone V.
- `verwalino-mark-dark.svg`: light standalone V.
- `verwalino-app-icon.svg`: 1024 × 1024 ink tile with a paper V, about 62% wide.
  The mark is optically centred: 8 units left and 8 down from its bounding-box centre.

All SVGs use outlined paths, plain hex fills and a viewBox without fixed dimensions.

## Size and clear space

Keep the wordmark at least 90 px wide and 20 px high; keep the standalone mark at
least 16 px. Leave clear space equal to the height of the V on all sides of the logo.
The app-icon tile uses its supplied composition.

## Colour and reproduction

Use ink `#232724` on paper `#F3F4F2`, or paper on ink. In dark themes, use light ink
`#EFF1E9` on dark paper `#1C2420`. Signal is `#B62930` in light themes and `#F18280`
in dark themes: use it only as an accent next to the mark, or as a tile behind a
paper mark.

The logo is single colour by construction. Black on white and white on black are
also allowed. Do not add outlines, gradients, other recolouring, rotation or house
shapes.

## Lettering

The lettering is derived from Outfit, licensed under the SIL Open Font License
(OFL). The logo is outlines; no font is needed to display it.
